Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about LinguaCheck

You need a computer with a front-facing camera, a microphone, a stable internet connection, and a valid government-issued ID. The test can be taken on Windows or Mac.

Your certified LinguaCheck score is valid for 2 years from the date you take the test. You can retake the test at any time if you want to improve your score.

The test evaluates all four core English language skills: Reading, Writing, Listening, and Speaking. Each skill is scored individually and combined into an overall proficiency score.

Scores range from 10 to 160, mapped to CEFR levels (A1-C2). The adaptive algorithm ensures precise scoring by adjusting question difficulty based on your performance.
